# Arion Health Portal

## Purpose
Arion Health Portal is a clinic management and patient portal system.

## Roles
- Patient
- Doctor
- Staff
- Admin

## Core MVP

### Patient
- Register/login
- Book appointment
- View appointments
- View medical records
- View medical certificates
- Manage profile

### Doctor
- View schedule
- View patient information
- Create medical records
- Issue medical certificates

### Staff
- Manage appointments
- Check in patients
- Manage queue
- Register walk-in patients

### Admin
- Manage doctors
- Manage staff
